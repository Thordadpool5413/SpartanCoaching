Spartan Coaching Field Kit

The private field companion for hospice growth professionals — clients and approved evaluators of Spartan Coaching.

The Field Kit puts your Spartan playbook in your pocket. Before a visit, during a debrief, or between stops — structured tools and drills that help you prepare referral conversations in real time. Coaching aid only. Do not enter PHI.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• AI Scenario Coach — Practice tough referral conversations with an AI role-play partner. Get feedback on approach and language.

• Branch Profitability Calculator — Planning model for territory and account math. Educational only — not a guarantee of results.

• Field Drills — Short, focused practice on objections, rapport, and census-growth tactics from the Spartan method.

• Visit Checklist — Structure field days: pre-call prep, post-visit notes, and follow-ups.

• Coaching Portal — Membership resources and progress for active clients and evaluators.

WHO IT'S FOR

Field Kit access is for Spartan Coaching clients and approved evaluators. Individuals can create an account and subscribe at spartanhospicecoaching.com. Teams and evaluations request access through Nick.

This is not a general consumer app — it is a professional coaching tool for hospice growth work.

PRIVACY

Field Kit tools are for planning and messaging — never patient identifiers or clinical records. Do not enter PHI. Clinical vault tools (when authorized) are educational decision support only, not diagnosis or coverage determinations. AI conversations are not sold to third parties. Operated by Spartan Coaching.
