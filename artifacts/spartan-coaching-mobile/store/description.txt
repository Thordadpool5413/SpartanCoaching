Spartan Coaching Membership

Tools and resources for hospice growth professionals — web and iPhone. Consulting stays human; membership unlocks Command Center, practice tools, plans, and calculators.

Before a visit, during a debrief, or between stops — structured tools and drills that help you prepare referral conversations in real time. Coaching aid only. Do not enter PHI.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Sales Command Center — Daily spine: plan the call, practice, capture the outcome, lock the next step.

• AI Scenario Coach — Practice tough referral conversations with an AI role-play partner. Get feedback on approach and language.

• Branch Profitability Calculator — Planning model for territory and account math. Educational only — not a guarantee of results.

• Field Drills — Short, focused practice on objections, rapport, and census-growth tactics from the Spartan method.

• Visit Checklist — Structure field days: pre-call prep, post-visit notes, and follow-ups.

• Membership Portal — Next action, tools, and resources for active members and evaluators.

WHO IT'S FOR

Hospice Sales Pro is for hospice growth professionals who want execution tools between coaching sessions — or on their own. Individuals create an account and subscribe ($14.99/week, cancel anytime) at spartanhospicecoaching.com. Teams and evaluations request access through Nick. Consulting engagements are separate.

This is not a general consumer app — it is a professional coaching tool for hospice growth work.

PRIVACY

Membership tools are for planning and messaging — never patient identifiers or clinical records. Do not enter PHI. Clinical vault tools (when authorized) are educational decision support only, not diagnosis or coverage determinations. AI conversations are not sold to third parties. Operated by Spartan Coaching.
