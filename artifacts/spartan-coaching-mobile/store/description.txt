Spartan Coaching Field Kit

The private field companion for hospice census growth — built for reps coached by Spartan Coaching.

The Field Kit puts your entire Spartan playbook in your pocket. Before a visit, during a debrief, or between stops — get the AI-powered tools and structured drills that sharpen your referral conversations in real time.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• AI Scenario Coach — Practice the toughest referral conversations with an AI that plays the referral source. Get real-time feedback on your approach, language, and close.

• Branch Profitability Calculator — Run the numbers on any territory or account. Show referral sources their actual patient flow value in seconds.

• Field Drills — Short, focused reps on objection handling, rapport building, and census growth tactics from the Spartan method.

• Visit Checklist — Structure every field day. Log pre-call prep, post-visit notes, and follow-up commitments so nothing falls through the cracks.

• Coaching Portal — Access your trial or active membership resources, track your progress, and stay connected to your coaching cohort.

WHO IT'S FOR

Field Kit access is available to Spartan Coaching clients and approved evaluators. Request access at spartanhospicecoaching.com or through your Spartan coach.

This is not a general consumer app — it is a professional tool for hospice census growth reps working with Spartan Coaching methodology.

PRIVACY

All AI conversations stay within your session. No conversation data is sold or shared with third parties. Field Kit is operated by Spartan Coaching.
