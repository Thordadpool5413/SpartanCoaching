Spartan Coaching gives hospice growth professionals a focused field system for the work between visits.

Open the app and know what to do next. Prepare for the conversation, rehearse the hard part, capture a commitment, and carry the Spartan Method with you.

TODAY

Start with one clear field briefing and one next move. Your priorities, visits, and commitments stay focused instead of becoming another dashboard to manage.

PRIVATE SPARTAN COACH

Prepare a real conversation by voice or text. Rehearse the opening, review suggested coaching, and save only the commitments that matter. Raw conversations remain private. Only summaries and commitments you explicitly share can be visible to a manager.

PRACTICE

Work through objections, role play, account planning, outreach, grounded research, and weekly planning. Each tool is designed to help you leave with a useful next action.

LIBRARY

Keep Spartan Coaching articles, audio briefings, field resources, drills, and the Spartan Method within reach.

MEMBERSHIPS

PRIVACY AND RESPONSIBLE USE

Do not enter patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, contact details, documents, or other patient PHI.

Clinical education and AI generated content are suggestions only. Every output requires review and approval by the hospice medical director, compliance leader, or both before use. Spartan Coaching does not make eligibility, treatment, admission, or patient care decisions.
