# About Spartan Coaching

## What We Do

Spartan Coaching is a professional coaching and consulting service built specifically for hospice growth professionals — the sales liaisons, market development reps, and organizational leaders responsible for expanding patient access to hospice care.

We exist to close the conversational gap.

Hundreds of thousands of Americans die each year without hospice care who would have qualified for it. The average hospice length of stay hovers around eighteen days. The Medicare benefit allows up to six months. That gap does not exist because of bad clinical decisions. It exists because the right conversations did not happen — a referral that did not get made, a physician who said "not yet" to a rep who did not know how to respond, an eligible patient who never got asked.

When a rep does the work well, that changes. A patient stops managing their own pain alone. A family gets a care team instead of a crisis. A daughter gets to be a daughter again instead of a medical coordinator trying to figure out what to do next. That is what good sales execution produces — not a quota hit, not a commission — a human being who gets comfort care they needed and deserved, earlier in their journey.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Most hospice sales teams struggle not because their people lack heart — they struggle because they lack structure. Common symptoms include:

- A calendar full of visits but a pipeline that stays flat
- Follow up falling through the cracks every week
- Objections that stall conversations the rep should be winning
- Territory planning that lives in a spreadsheet nobody updates
- New reps taking months to produce while experienced reps plateau

The issue is not motivation. It is the absence of a repeatable system — one that tells a rep where to go, who to see, and what to say, week after week, regardless of how hard the week gets.

- **Referral source segmentation** — Structured approach for hospitals, SNFs, home health, physicians, senior living, and community partners

- **Objection Handler** — Expert responses to the most common hospice-specific hesitations
- **Activity Calculator** — Convert monthly admission goals into daily and weekly conversation targets, including ramp plans for new hires
