COLD CALL OPENING SCRIPT
Professional Guide for Hospice Sales

OPENING (15-20 seconds):
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Company]. I know you're busy, so I'll be brief. 
We work with facilities like yours to improve patient outcomes and family satisfaction by 
connecting eligible patients with specialized hospice care earlier in their journey. 
Do you have 30 seconds?"

KEY POINTS TO ESTABLISH:
• You understand their role and challenges
• You bring specific value (outcomes, satisfaction, referral process)
• You're respectful of their time

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS:
1. "How many patients in your facility would you say are appropriate for hospice?"
2. "What's your current referral process when a patient becomes appropriate?"
3. "Who else is involved in those decisions with you?"
4. "What's the biggest challenge you face in those situations?"

OBJECTION HANDLING:

If "We already have a hospice partner":
"I respect that. Many facilities work with multiple partners to ensure best outcomes. 
Would you be open to a brief conversation about how our approach differs?"

If "Not interested right now":
"Understood. The reason I called is many facilities don't realize they're missing eligible 
patients. Could I send you our quick assessment? Takes 5 minutes to review."

If "We don't have time":
"Perfect, that's exactly why I'm calling—to save you time. Can I schedule 15 minutes 
next week when you have availability?"

CLOSING:
"Great, let's set up a time to chat more. Would Tuesday or Wednesday work better?"

FOLLOW-UP TIMING:
• If no commitment: Call back in 2-3 weeks
• If commitment made: Send materials within 24 hours
• After send: Follow up in 3-5 days

SUCCESS METRICS:
✓ 30+ calls per week
✓ 15-20% conversation rate
✓ 5-8% meeting rate
✓ Track objections for coaching
