MEDICARE/MEDICAID HOSPICE REGULATIONS QUICK REFERENCE
Essential Compliance Information for Sales Professionals

MEDICARE HOSPICE ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
1. Eligible Patient Criteria:
   ✓ Must be enrolled in Medicare Part A
   ✓ Physician must certify terminal illness
   ✓ Prognosis: 6 months or less if disease runs normal course
   ✓ Patient must sign consent form

2. Conditions Commonly Eligible:
   • Terminal cancer
   • COPD with specific markers
   • Heart failure (NYHA Class IV)
   • Renal disease (creatinine >2.5)
   • Liver disease (albumin <2.5)
   • ALS
   • Dementia/Alzheimer's (specific criteria)
   • Stroke/severe brain injury

3. Non-Terminal Conditions (Not Eligible):
   ✗ Diabetes without complications
   ✗ Hypertension alone
   ✗ Early-stage cancer without systemic involvement
   ✗ Stable chronic disease without acute decline

REFERRAL PROCESS REQUIREMENTS:
Step 1: Physician Evaluation
- Physician must evaluate patient and certify terminal status
- Must complete detailed medical documentation

Step 2: Patient Consent
- Patient (or representative) must consent to hospice care
- Must sign Medicare Hospice Election Form (CMS-1525)
- Waives curative treatment coverage under Medicare

Step 3: Formal Admission
- Patient must be admitted within specific timeframe
- Initial assessment completed
- Plan of care developed

Step 4: Documentation
- Certification signed by two physicians
- Prognosis documented
- Plan of care on file

COMMON REFERRAL BARRIERS & SOLUTIONS:
Barrier: "Patient not ready to give up curative care"
Solution: Frame hospice as complementary support, pain management, quality of life

Barrier: "Physician hasn't formally certified terminal status"
Solution: Provide physician with hospice eligibility criteria and guidelines

Barrier: "Family hesitant about enrollment"
Solution: Explain benefits, trial period available, can revoke if desired

Barrier: "Insurance issues"
Solution: Verify Medicare Part A coverage, check secondary insurance

RED FLAGS IN REFERRAL PROCESS:
🚩 Physician certification without patient contact
🚩 Pressure to enroll inappropriate patients
🚩 Incentives offered for referrals
🚩 Lack of proper documentation
🚩 Non-physician making hospice decisions
🚩 Patient enrollment without true consent
