That is the problem Spartan Coaching was built to solve.

An eligible patient who never got asked.

When a rep does the work well, that changes. A family gets a care team instead of a crisis. A patient stops managing pain alone. A daughter gets to be a daughter again.

That is what good hospice sales execution produces. Not just a quota hit — a human being who gets the care they needed, earlier in their journey.
